Kitten Alarm System Circuit Design

This document outlines the step-by-step design of a digital logic circuit for a kitten feeding alarm system.

(i) Truth Table

The circuit inputs are the 4-bit binary representation of time (T3, T2, T1, T0), and the output is a single bit (Z) indicating whether the alarm should ring. The alarm rings at 3, 6, 9, and 12 o'clock. The numbers 1-12 are valid inputs, while 0 and 13-15 are don't care conditions.

Decimal Time T3 T2 T1 T0 (Input) Z (Output) Condition
0 0000 X Don't Care
1 0001 0 No Alarm
2 0010 0 No Alarm
3 0011 1 Alarm Rings
4 0100 0 No Alarm
5 0101 0 No Alarm
6 0110 1 Alarm Rings
7 0111 0 No Alarm
8 1000 0 No Alarm
9 1001 1 Alarm Rings
10 1010 0 No Alarm
11 1011 0 No Alarm
12 1100 1 Alarm Rings
13 1101 X Don't Care
14 1110 X Don't Care
15 1111 X Don't Care

(ii) Simplified Expression for the Output Bit

By analyzing the truth table, we can identify the minterms where the output Z is 1. The inputs corresponding to decimal 3, 6, 9, and 12 produce an output of 1. When mapping these minterms onto a 4-variable Karnaugh map (K-map), we find that they are not adjacent to each other, nor can they be grouped with the "don't care" conditions to form larger, simpler product terms.

Therefore, the simplified Sum-of-Products (SOP) expression is the sum of the minterms for which the output is 1. Using T3, T2, T1, and T0 as the input variables:

Z = T3'T2'T1T0 + T3'T2T1T0' + T3T2'T1'T0 + T3T2T1'T0'

(iii) PLA Implementation

The simplified expression can be implemented using a Programmable Logic Array (PLA), which consists of a programmable AND array and a programmable OR array. The PLA will have 4 inputs (T3, T2, T1, T0), and their complements. There will be 4 product term lines, each corresponding to one of the minterms from the simplified expression. These product terms are then fed into the single output line through the OR array. The connections (indicated by 'X's) are made at the intersections of the input lines and product term lines in the AND array, and between the product term lines and the output line in the OR array, as per the simplified expression.
