3. Logic equations

The full adder outputs can be expressed as:

  • Sum: S = A ⊕ B ⊕ Cin
  • Carry-out: Cout = (A & B) | (Cin & (A ⊕ B))

Alternate (expanded) boolean form for Cout: Cout = A·B + A·Cin + B·Cin.

Figure: Logical block diagram (conceptual) for a 1-bit full adder using XOR, AND and OR blocks. In Logisim you will use XOR, AND and OR components wired exactly as the equations above.

How it works (short): XOR1 produces A ⊕ B. XOR2 then combines that with Cin to produce S. AND(A,B) and AND(Cin, A⊕B) detect cases that produce a carry; OR combines them to produce Cout.

7. Conclusions

The full adder is a fundamental digital building block. Using XORs for the Sum and a combination of AND/OR gates for Cout yields a compact implementation. Multi-bit adders are built by cascading 1-bit adders (ripple-carry); while simple, they have linear delay with the number of bits due to carry propagation.

For faster adders (beyond this lab): look into carry-lookahead adders or carry-select adders (advanced topics).

8. Appendix — Boolean simplification & Derivation

Start from S = A ⊕ B ⊕ Cin. For Cout: Cout = majority(A,B,Cin). Expanding: Cout = A·B + A·Cin + B·Cin. Using the A⊕B helper, Cout = (A·B) + (Cin & (A ⊕ B)).
